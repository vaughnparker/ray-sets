The 16 ray-sets

Every twisty-puzzle axis system with polyhedral symmetry — a bundle of rays from the centre, the directions you can turn around.

Why these are special. Spin the whole bundle around any one of its own rays and it lands exactly back on itself — every arrow ends where another began. A random handful of arrows won't do that: some ray would swing into empty space and the puzzle would jam. These 16 are the only polyhedral bundles that pass the test.

Or click any ray in the view to spin the bundle once around it and watch it land back on itself.
